# Research notes: Kelly Clarkson vs. Art of Play ("Molly" the bird)

Compiled 2026-10-15. Calendar check: Oct 10, 2026 = Saturday, Oct 11 = Sunday, Oct 12 = Monday, Oct 13 = Tuesday, Oct 14 = Wednesday.

## What happened (verified, multiple outlets)

| Date | Event | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| Sat Oct 10 | Clarkson visits Art of Play, a magic, puzzle and games shop on Atlantic Ave, Brooklyn. Per the security footage she is inside for about 4 min 50 s and leaves without buying anything. | TODAY, Just Jared, TMZ (footage) |
| Oct 10 | She posts on X that she drove to Brooklyn to buy several things, felt judged on whether she could afford them, was steered to "something comparable", and compares it to *Pretty Woman*. The post passes 32M views. | Rolling Stone, HuffPost, Fox |
| Sun Oct 11 | Art of Play on Instagram: she asked about **Molly**, a one-of-a-kind singing/dancing bird automaton used in its magic shows that is "permanently attached to the wall and isn't for sale." | Complex, TODAY, HuffPost |
| Oct 11 | Clarkson replies sarcastically ("Sure, it wasn't for sale…"), shares a photo of a tag she says shows $15,000, and posts "So you're not only rude, you're also a liar." | NBC, Complex, THR |
| Mon Oct 12 | Store clarification: she was in for about 5 minutes. A near-identical **twin** of Molly is owned by Seattle artist **Bliss Kolb**, who would sell it for $15,000. Staff also offered a ~$2,000 bird. | NBC, Complex |
| Tue Oct 13 | Pablo Torre ("Pablo Torre Finds Out") releases silent CCTV of the visit. Co-owner **Adam Rubin** talks to press: "Kelly Clarkson has put us in danger", "an insane misunderstanding", reports threats, harassment and review-bombing, invites her back to a magic show. He also says Molly is now "for sale" at **$15 million**. | TODAY, NBC, Curbed via Billboard, TMZ, News12 |
| Wed Oct 14 | Rubin declines further comment. Clarkson and Kolb have not responded to NBC. | NBC |

## Confirmed
- The visit happened, it lasted under 5 minutes, and she left without buying.
- The CCTV has **no audio**. It shows her browsing, testing a kaleidoscope, looking at Molly, talking with an employee and pointing at the bird, walking toward the exit, then returning to talk with a cashier (ABC description).
- A $15,000 figure came up in the conversation. **Both sides agree on this**; they disagree about *what* it referred to.

## Disputed
- Was Molly for sale? The store says no and that $15k referred to the twin. Clarkson says she was quoted $15k for Molly, and shows a tag.
- Was she treated rudely or "elevator-eyed"? This is her perception against the store's "treated like a normal person." **The footage can't settle it because it has no audio.**
- Rubin says she scolded the shopkeeper on the way out. This is his claim and is unverified.

## Opinion / analysis (for the script, labelled as opinion)
- The CCTV proves almost nothing about the actual dispute. A PR expert told TODAY the footage only verifies she was in the store, which nobody questioned.
- This is a power imbalance: one post with 32M views against a small shop with a handful of staff. Harassment of staff is a real consequence, whoever is "right".
- "Not for sale" plus a $15k twin plus a "comparable" $2k bird is a recipe for a genuine misunderstanding. Both versions can be partly true.
- The $15M price tag shows the store also learned how to play the attention game.

## Careful wording rules
- Say "she says" / "the store says" for every disputed point. Never call either side a liar.
- Don't name or show the employee. Blur any face other than Clarkson's and Rubin's (Rubin speaks publicly to press).
- No claim of racism or profiling: one creator alleged this about the store, but it is unsupported. Leave it out.
