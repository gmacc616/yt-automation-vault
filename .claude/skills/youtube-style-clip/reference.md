# Evidence behind /youtube-style-clip

Collected 2026-10-18 by studying the top BOXABL campaign Shorts in the built-in browser. Frames were scrubbed and checked by eye. Audio and video were measured in the page with Web Audio loudness analysis and frame-difference cut detection.

**Limits:** the audio was never heard. Sound-effect hits were inferred from sudden loudness jumps, and cut counts may include caption-word changes. All findings are correlation, not causation; topic, celebrity and upload age are mixed in with the editing.

## Patterns shared by the winners
- **Length:** 10-17 s, ending on the payoff, so viewers watch to the end and replay. Every replay counts as a view since 2025-03-31: https://ppc.land/youtube-changes-how-shorts-views-are-counted-from-march-31/
- **Layout:** a black top bar with a bold white headline, a red/yellow keyword and an emoji; footage fills the rest of the 9:16 frame.
- **Captions:** single words mid-frame, yellow/green/white with an outline, synced to the original speakers.
- **Graphics:** red arrows or circles on the car or the famous face; celebrity cutaways (Musk interview, IShowSpeed reaction).
- **Look:** hard cuts; light-leak or white flashes only at big scene changes or the final impact; punch-in zooms on faces; one saturated grade.
- **Titles:** a challenge or question ("Could Elon Musk's house SURVIVE this!?", "Would you live…?").

## Platform rules that shape the style (researched 2026-10-18)
- **YouTube reused content** (https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392):
  - Monetizable: clips with commentary or storyline.
  - Not monetizable: clips "edited together with little or no narrative" and mostly non-verbal reactions.
  - "Repetitious" was renamed "inauthentic content" on 2025-07-15 and targets templated, mass-produced videos.
- **Instagram (April 2026):** stops recommending accounts that repost content without "meaningful transformation" (https://planoly.com/blog/instagram-updates-its-original-content-policy). Watermarks from other apps get demoted.
- **Campaign "no AI" rules** usually ban text-to-speech narration and generated visuals; basic captioning is usually fine (https://docs.jestr.gg/core-concepts/campaign-guidelines). Always confirm in the brief.

## Other campaign context
- The user's SnapRush YouTube audience is mostly in the Philippines (72.9% as of 2026-10-18). Campaigns that require 50%+ US/UK/CA/AU/NZ audiences (e.g. the Reach campaigns) are a poor fit until that changes.
- Reality-TV and streamer campaigns follow the same "single moment, no context needed" rule. Example: a Love Island USA clip passed 43M TikTok views (https://www.michiganpublic.org/2025-07-14/why-the-latest-season-of-love-island-usa-became-the-internets-obsession).
