# Effect selection guide (workflow step 4)

Choose from the footage, the reference and the story beat, never at random. Order: the user's prompt → campaign rules →
the user's standing rules (fixed opening/riser/loop; a **different** transition at every scene change) → the reference's
measured style → this guide. Quick cuts inside one scene stay hard cuts.

| Moment | Transition into it | Overlay / caption | Sound (catalog) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strong opening statement | (fixed opening zoom-out) | `punch-in` caption | fixed woosh, then an impact on the key word |
| Surprising reveal | `flash-impact`, `white-flash`, `split-reveal` | `freeze-frame` or `spotlight` | riser (hook only) → boom / cinematic hit |
| Important statistic or price | `punch-zoom`, `snap-zoom` | `counter` (dated "(2023)" if the brief needs it), `progress-bar` | money / data sounds, one restrained hit |
| Funny reaction | `snap-zoom`, `spin`, `shake-cut` | `speech-bubble`, `shake` caption | comedic sound (user folder, not in campaign clips if branded) |
| Fast scene / location change | `whip`, `push`, `slide` | `slide-in` / `slide-up` captions | woosh, swish |
| Tech, futuristic, digital | `glitch`, `rgb-split`, `breakup`, `static` | `typewriter`, `blur-in` captions | glitch / digital SFX |
| Emotional or reflective | `focus-pull`, `light-leak`, `blur-to-clear` | `fade-rise` captions, `vignette-pulse` | soft shimmer or none |
| Podcast / commentary | mostly hard cuts; `punch-zoom` on key lines | `underline`, `label`, `lower-third` for names | few SFX |
| Business, architecture, product (BOXABL) | `push-in`, `pull-out`, `wipe`, `diagonal-wipe`, `circle-reveal` | `box`, `label`, `lower-third` | clean whooshes, shutter on reveals |
| Retro / throwback | `vhs`, `exposure` | `flip` captions | tape / static |
| "Meanwhile" or reaction to footage | `pip-in` | `notification` | notification ping |
| Speed, action, sport | `zoom-blur`, `radial-blur`, `zoom-in` | `stretch-in` captions | whoosh into impact |

Avoid: a transition on every quick cut, constant shake, stacked flashes, captions that cover faces or the subject,
the same sound repeated back to back, and anything the campaign brief bans (e.g. BOXABL: never crop the whole-home shot
with a reveal; DraftKings: ≤2 SFX besides the fixed ones, which the user has made mandatory; flag conflicts).
