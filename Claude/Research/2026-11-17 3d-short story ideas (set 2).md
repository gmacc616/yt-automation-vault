# /3d-short Stage 0: 10 researched stories (2026-11-17, set 2)
Excludes the 2026-11-16 and 2026-11-17 lists and the 20 RicksClips3000 titles.

1. **Tsar Bell, Moscow 1737.** Trinity Fire; cold water on the hot bell (most common account) cracked off ~11.5 t piece; never rung; raised 1836. Disputed cause. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsar_Bell https://kremlin-architectural-ensemble.kreml.ru/en-Us/the-tsar-bell
2. **Red Flag Act, Britain 1865.** 4 mph country / 2 mph town, crew of 3, man with red flag 60 yd ahead; flag dropped 1878; 14 mph in 1896. Documented. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locomotive_Acts https://www.roads.org.uk/articles/limit/not-so-fast
3. **Roman self-healing concrete.** MIT/Masic, Science Advances 2023: hot-mixed quicklime clasts; cracks refill with calcium carbonate; lab cracks sealed ~2 weeks. Documented study. https://sustainability.mit.edu/article/riddle-solved-why-was-roman-concrete-so-durable
4. **Pig War, San Juan Island 1859.** Cutlar shot an HBC pig in his potatoes (15 Jun); troop/warship standoff; Kaiser Wilhelm I arbitrated 1872; pig the only casualty. Documented. https://www.historylink.org/File/5037 https://www.historic-uk.com/HistoryUK/HistoryofBritain/The-Pig-War/
5. **Mechanical Turk, 1770.** Kempelen's chess "automaton" hid a human player on a sliding seat; toured 84 yrs; Napoleon 1809; burned 1854. Documented hoax; anecdotes colourful. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_Turk https://www.history.com/articles/how-a-phony-18th-century-chess-robot-fooled-the-world
6. **Teflon, DuPont 1938.** Plunkett/Rebok: TFE cylinder gave no gas but weighed full; sawed open → white PTFE powder. Documented. https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/roy-plunkett https://www.smithsonianmag.com/science-nature/the-long-strange-history-of-teflon-the-indestructible-product-nothing-seems-to-stick-to-180984920/
7. **Great Fire of London, 1666.** Mayor Bludworth delayed firebreaks ("a woman might piss it out", popular quote, wording unverified); gunpowder firebreaks from day 3 + wind drop ended it. Documented; quote Disputed. https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/topics/great-fire-london https://www.history.com/articles/when-london-burned-1666s-great-fire
8. **Edinburgh One O'Clock Gun, 1861.** Time ball (1852-53) for ship chronometers hidden by haar; gun added, wire-synced to the ball's clock. Documented; first-firing date varies. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_Monument,_Edinburgh https://blog.edinburghcastle.scot/?p=1103
9. **Ice King Frederic Tudor, 1806.** Shipped New England ice to Martinique; laughed at, early loss; sawdust packing later built a global trade. Documented; cargo size varies. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederic_Tudor https://www.hagley.org/librarynews/patent=model-ice-harvesting
10. **Wojtek the bear, 1944.** Enlisted as a private to ship to Italy (pets banned); reportedly carried shell crates at Monte Cassino; made corporal. Enlistment documented; carrying Disputed. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wojtek_(bear) https://www.britannica.com/print/article/2226582
