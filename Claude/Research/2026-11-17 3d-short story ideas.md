# /3d-short Stage 0: 10 researched stories (2026-11-17)
Excluded: the 2026-11-16 list ([[2026-11-16 3d-short story ideas]]), the bridge, the Vasa, and the 20 RicksClips3000 topics.

1. **Lake Peigneur, Louisiana 1980.** 20 Nov 1980, a Texaco-contracted rig drilling in the lake punctured the salt mine below; the lake drained into the mine in ~3 h, the whirlpool swallowed the rig, a tug and ~10 barges and 65 acres of shore; the canal reversed and refilled it with salt water. All ~55 miners escaped by the lift; no deaths. Documented (miner count 50-55; MSHA never fixed the blame: wrong spot vs wrong mine maps). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Peigneur · https://www.amusingplanet.com/2022/04/the-lake-peigneur-drilling-disaster.html · https://library.louisiana.edu/node/2548
2. **Great Molasses Flood, Boston 1919.** Tank burst; a wave ~25 ft at ~35 mph; cold air quadrupled the viscosity so it set like glue (Harvard/APS 2016 study). 21 died: needs a non-graphic telling. Documented (wave height 25-40 ft disputed). https://www.livescience.com/56947-why-molasses-flood-1919-was-deadly.html · https://www.cbc.ca/radio/asithappens/as-it-happen-friday-edition-1.3867938/science-explains-what-made-the-boston-molasses-flood-of-1919-so-deadly-1.3867940
3. **Dagen H, Sweden 1967.** 3 Sep 1967, 05:00, the whole country switched from left to right; 157 minor accidents that day, fewer than a normal Sunday; Monday 125 vs usual 130-198. Documented (4:50 stop time single-source). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dagen_H · https://www.mentalfloss.com/history/day-everyone-sweden-had-start-driving-other-side-road
4. **Big Ben's pennies.** Pre-1971 pennies on the pendulum: each lifts the centre of mass, shortens the effective length, clock gains 0.4 s/day. Documented (Parliament). https://www.britannica.com/topic/Big-Ben-clock-London · https://webtest.parliament.uk/business/news/news-by-year/2015/august/great-clock-adjustments
5. **Tambora → Drais's running machine, 1815-1817.** Eruption → 1816 Year Without a Summer → oat prices x8, horses sold/starved → Drais's 1817 wooden two-wheeler. Disputed (eruption, harvests, oats solid; the causal link to Drais is unproven). https://www.earthdate.org/node/290 · https://www.ichc.biz/proceedings/ICHC-033-06.pdf · https://karldrais.de/kd-en-overview-climatic_catastrophe.html
6. **Eratosthenes measures the Earth, ~240 BC.** Sun to the bottom of a Syene well at solstice noon; Alexandria stick shadow 7.2° = 1/50 circle; 5,000 stadia x 50 = 250,000 stadia. Documented method (stadion length disputed, so accuracy 2-16 %). https://www.aps.org/apsnews/2006/06/eratosthenes-measures-earth · https://www.lindahall.org/about/news/scientist-of-the-day/eratosthenes-of-cyrene
7. **Project Pigeon, 1940s.** B.F. Skinner trained 3 pigeons in a missile nose ("Pelican") to peck the target image; NDRC $25,000; dropped 1944, revived as Orcon 1948-53. Documented (steering details vary). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Pigeon · https://www.army.mil/article/111511/its_a_bird_its_a_plane
8. **Percy Spencer's microwave, 1945-46.** Raytheon magnetron; candy bar melted in his pocket; popcorn test; patent filed 8 Oct 1945; Radarange 1947 ~6 ft, ~750 lb. Documented invention, candy bar is Legend-ish (no primary source). https://spectrum.ieee.org/a-history-of-the-microwave-oven · https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/percy-spencer
9. **Exploding whale, Florence, Oregon 1970.** 45 ft, ~8 t sperm whale; Highway Division used ~half a ton of dynamite; blubber rained down, a chunk crushed a car roof; most of the whale stayed; now Exploding Whale Memorial Park. Documented (car make/location vary). https://www.popsci.com/science/exploding-whale/ · https://www.mentalfloss.com/history/florence-oregon-exploding-whale-1970
10. **Beauvais Cathedral, 1284.** Tallest Gothic choir ever (~46.7-48.5 m); 12 years after completion the vault and some flying buttresses collapsed; rebuilt with extra piers. Documented (height varies by source; death claims uncorroborated). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beauvais_Cathedral · https://cathedrale-beauvais.fr/en/historical-overview-of-beauvais-cathedral/
