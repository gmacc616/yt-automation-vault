# /inkbeat-shorts: 10 researched topic ideas (2026-11-19, set 1)
One per category; fact-checked with web searches (excludes the 40 3d-short stories).
1. **The 10 days that never happened** (History). Gregorian reform 1582: Thu 4 Oct → Fri 15 Oct; Britain only 1752. Documented. https://www.britannica.com/story/ten-days-that-vanished-the-switch-to-the-gregorian-calendar
2. **The IKEA effect** (Psychology). Norton, Mochon, Ariely 2012: self-built items valued higher; gone if not finished or taken apart. Documented. https://dash.harvard.edu/handle/1/12136084
3. **The book nobody can read** (Mystery). Voynich MS 408, parchment 1404-1438 (Arizona C14, 2009); unsolved. https://collections.library.yale.edu/catalog/2002046
4. **Wrinkly bath fingers** (Human body). Nerve-driven vasoconstriction; Newcastle 2013 grip advantage, Berlin replication none. Disputed. https://www.ncl.ac.uk/press/articles/archive/2013/01/getagrip-whywegetwrinklyfingersandtoes.html
5. **Venus's day is longer than its year** (Space). Rotation ~243 d, orbit ~225 d, retrograde; solar day ~117 d. Documented. https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/all-about-venus/
6. **The animal that poops cubes** (Animals). Wombat gut stiffness bands (Yang, Hu et al., Soft Matter 2021). Documented. https://www.rsc.org/news-events/articles/2021/jan/wombat-poo/
7. **Tomato pills: ketchup as medicine, myth vs truth** (Food). Bennett 1830s; Miles' pills ~1837, tomato pill war; mostly laxatives. Disputed framing. https://daily.jstor.org/tomatoes-as-medicine
8. **Handling salmon in suspicious circumstances** (Weird laws). Salmon Act 1986 s.32, anti-poaching; widened 2009. Documented. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1986/62/section/32/enacted
9. **Baarle: your front door picks your country** (Geography). ~22 Belgian + 8 Dutch enclaves; front-door rule. Documented. https://www.visitbrabant.com/en/locations/1472222069/enclavedorp-baarle-hertog-nassau
10. **The spring that walked off a shelf** (Lucky accidents). Richard James 1943, Slinky, Gimbels 1945. Documented. https://lemelson.mit.edu/resources/richard-james
