# /3d-short Stage 0: 10 researched stories (2026-11-16)

Checked against the 20 reference shorts (Claude/Research/RicksClips3000 transcripts.md) and our own Broughton bridge short: none reused.

1. **Vasa, Stockholm 1628.** Stability test: ~30 men ran side to side, stopped after ~3 runs for fear of capsizing; sailed anyway 10 Aug 1628, sank ~1.3 km out via open gunports. Documented (test details vary). https://the-past.com/shorts/ideas/back-to-the-drawing-board-vasa/ · https://www.irishtimes.com/world/europe/2023/05/16/the-vasa-was-one-of-the-most-powerful-ships-in-the-world-for-about-20-minutes
2. **Leaning Tower of Pisa, 1178.** Leaned after 3rd storey; ~century-long pause (wars) let clay consolidate, likely saved it; 1990-2001 soil extraction. Documented / "saved it" is expert interpretation. https://ingenia.org.uk/articles/the-stabilisation-of-the-leaning-tower-of-pisa/ · https://geoengineer.org/education/web-class-projects/ce-179-geosystems-engineering-design/assignments/the-tilt-of-the-tower-of-pisa-why-and-how
3. **Great Stink, London 1858.** Curtains soaked in chloride of lime; Parliament funded Bazalgette's sewers within weeks. Documented. https://londonmuseum.org.uk/collections/london-stories/great-stink-of-1858 · https://www.historic-uk.com/HistoryUK/HistoryofBritain/Londons-Great-Stink/
4. **Gimli Glider, 1983.** lb/L density used instead of kg/L; ~half the fuel; glided to ex-RCAF Gimli, runway part drag strip; no deaths. Documented. https://www.cbc.ca/archives/when-a-metric-mix-up-led-to-the-gimli-glider-emergency-1.4754039 · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gimli_Glider
5. **Emu War, 1932.** Meredith, 2 soldiers, 2 Lewis guns, 10,000 rounds vs ~20,000 emus; withdrawn. Documented (numbers approximate). https://www.nationalgeographic.com/history/article/australian-emu-war-history · https://www.britannica.com/topic/Emu-War
6. **Window tax, 1696-1851.** Bricked-up windows real; "daylight robbery" origin is legend. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Window_tax · https://www.mentalfloss.com/culture/architecture/bricked-up-windows-british-homes
7. **Egyptian wet sand.** Bonn, UvA 2014 (PRL): wet sand ~halves pull force; Djehutihotep painting. Documented experiment, disputed as the whole method. https://iop.uva.nl/content/news/2014/00/prl-egyptian-pyramids.html · https://www.livescience.com/45285-how-egyptians-moved-pyramid-stones.html
8. **Carrington event, 1859.** Boston-Portland telegraph ran on auroral current, batteries off. Documented; "two hours" unverified. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrington_Event · https://www.history.com/news/a-perfect-solar-superstorm-the-1859-carrington-event
9. **Fleming's mould, 1928.** Staph plates left out over holiday; mould cleared bacteria. Documented (dates vary). https://www.sciencehistory.org/historical-profile/alexander-fleming · https://www.livescience.com/health/science-history-alexander-fleming-wakes-up-to-funny-mold-in-his-petri-dish-and-accidentally-discovers-the-first-antibiotic-sept-28-1928
10. **Claw of Archimedes, Syracuse 213-212 BC.** Polybius/Livy: crane lifted Roman ship bows and dropped them. Disputed (ancient accounts only; modern tests show feasible). https://math.nyu.edu/Archimedes/Claw/sources.html · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siege_of_Syracuse_(213%E2%80%93212_BC)
