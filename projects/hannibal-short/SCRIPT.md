# The General Who Broke a Mountain — script + facts

## Facts (checked 2026-11-18)
- 218 BC, Second Punic War: Hannibal marched from Spain over the Alps into Italy with an army and 37 elephants (Polybius 3.42; Livy 21).
- Livy 21.35: nine days to the summit, two days' rest there, then a fresh snowfall on the descent; men and pack animals slipping.
- Livy 21.36: a landslip had carried the track away for about 1,000 (Roman) feet; detours failed on fresh snow over old ice.
- Livy 21.37: they felled huge trees, piled them against the rock and fired them; "when the rock was red hot they poured vinegar upon it", then cut it with iron tools and made zigzags so even the elephants could be led down. Four days at the rock; the animals nearly starved (little grass under the snow). Then three days down to the plains (21.37-38); 15 days in the Alps overall (21.38, Polybius 3.56).
- Ambushes by the Allobroges and other Alpine tribes on the ascent (Polybius 3.50-53; Livy 21.33-34).
- Verdict: DISPUTED. Polybius (closer to the events) describes clearing the rockfall but not fire or vinegar; Appian, Pliny and Ammianus repeat vinegar. Fire-setting (heat, then quench, = thermal shock) is a real ancient/medieval mining method; acetic acid also attacks limestone, not granite. The script flags it: "At least, that's what the Roman historian Livy swears."

## Shot plan
Day (setup): aerial over snowy Alpine ridges, a long column of soldiers + elephants on a mountain path; Hannibal card (helmet, red cloak) leading; doubter soldiers. Climb montage: rocks bouncing down, wind. Summit + snow starts (sky goes grey). The broken path: a gap where the landslide swept the track, a giant boulder blocking the only way; slipping horse, stubborn elephant, soldier chewing snow. Night/storm crisis (blue): men felling trees, stacking logs at the rock, huge bonfire (warm key in blue night), rock glowing red; amphorae of vinegar poured, steam burst. "So how...?" mystery beat → night explanation: a kitchen-scale hot glass dish under a cold tap (insert), crack. Payoff golden/sunrise: rock cracks apart, zigzag path cut, column of elephants walking down into a sunny green Italian valley; last image the elephant in the snow, slow push.
