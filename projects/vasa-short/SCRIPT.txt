The most powerful warship in the world sank... before it even left the harbour!
Stockholm, sixteen twenty-eight. The King of Sweden wanted a ship that would terrify every navy on Earth. So they built Vasa. Sixty-four cannons. Golden lions and monsters carved everywhere.
There was just one tiny problem. Before she sailed, the admiral had thirty sailors run across the deck. Once... she leaned. Twice... she leaned more. Three times... and she rolled so hard he yelled, stop!
So did anyone fix it? Nope. Nobody wanted to tell the King.
August tenth. Crowds packed the shore. A little breeze filled the sails... and Vasa tipped. She came back up. Then a second gust... and water poured in through the open gunports.
Twenty minutes. Thirteen hundred metres. Gone.
Luckily, the shore was right there, so most of the crew made it. Unluckily, the shore was right there... so the whole city watched.
You see, a ship is like you, standing on a bus. Hold your shopping down low, and you ride out every bump. Hold it over your head... and one little turn sends you flying. Vasa carried her heavy guns way up high.
The inquiry asked who was to blame... and decided, nobody.
But here's the thing. The ship built to impress the world finally did. They pulled her up in nineteen sixty-one, and twenty-nine million people have visited since... and she still hasn't left Stockholm.
