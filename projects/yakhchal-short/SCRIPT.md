# The Desert Freezer (yakhchal) — script + facts

## Facts (checked 2026-11-18)
- Yakhchal ("ice pit"): Persian ice houses in the desert (Yazd, Kerman, Meybod, Abarkuh). Surviving ones date from the Safavid period on (Archnet); the popular "400 BC" origin is unproven, so the script only says "centuries ago".
- Ice pools: long channels (~100 m x 10 m), 40-50 cm deep, on the north (shaded) side of tall east-west shade walls (~10 m, up to 15 m); covered with straw by day; filled with qanat water on clear, dry winter nights; the water freezes by radiative cooling to the night sky (plus evaporation), even with the air slightly above freezing. (Wikipedia "Yakhchal"; Iranica "YAKČĀL")
- The Kerman (Moayedi) ice house: a cone-shaped building about 18 m (~59 ft) tall. Walls of sarooj (sand, clay, egg whites, lime, goat hair, ash), at least 2 m (6.5 ft) thick at the base. (Wikipedia)
- Before dawn the ice was broken up and stored in the deep pit under the dome, layers separated by straw; the tall cone vents warm air out of the top like a chimney; ice kept into summer, used to chill food and for desserts like faloodeh. (Wikipedia; Archnet)
- Summer in Yazd regularly passes 40 °C (104 °F).
- Verdict: DOCUMENTED (method and buildings); dates and "egg whites" are traditional accounts.
- Not used by the reference channel (checked against its 20 known titles).

Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakhch%C4%81l · https://archnet.org/sites/6442 · https://iranicaonline.org/articles/yakcal

## Shot plan
Hot golden-noon desert village (mud houses, windcatchers), a kid with a bowl of frozen faloodeh, heat shimmer; the dome rising; workers mixing mud with eggs + goat hair (doubter lines); winter: digging the long pool beside the tall shade wall, the wall's shadow; night (blue, stars): flooding the pool, frost spreading over the water by morning; mystery beat; the explanation at night: heat arrows rising from the water to the sky, then a kitchen-scale open freezer with someone standing in front of it; sunrise (golden): workers smashing ice, dragging blocks into the dome, stacking with straw in the pit; cut-away of the dome venting warm air; summer noon: the door opens, ice comes out, desserts; final: everyone eating, push up to the sky.
